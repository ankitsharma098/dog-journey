Getting started

How do I add my dog?

From Home, tap the + button and choose "Add dog." Only a name is required — you can fill in birthdate, breed, and a photo later from the Passport tab.

Can I track more than one dog?

Yes. Switch between dogs any time from the pill at the top of Home. The free plan covers one dog; PawJourney Pro covers every dog in your home under one account.

How does the breed scanner work?

Open Breed Scanner and point your camera at your dog. It reads the live camera feed to identify breed and common mixes — no photo library upload needed, though you can use a saved photo instead if your dog won't hold still.

Health records & reminders

Where do vaccines and medications live?

The Passport tab holds every record — vaccines, medications, weight checks, vet visits — in one timeline per dog, exportable as a PDF for a new vet or a boarder.

Will I actually get notified when something's due?

Yes. Set a due date on any record and PawJourney schedules a real notification on your device for that date — it's not just a number sitting in the Care calendar waiting to be checked.

I marked something done, but I'm still worried it'll remind me again.

Marking a due item "Done" from the Care calendar clears its due date and cancels the scheduled reminder together — nothing lingers on either side.

AI vet chat

Is the AI vet chat a replacement for a real veterinarian?

No. It's built to help you think through symptoms and know when something can wait until morning versus needs care now — and it's built to recognize real emergencies and tell you plainly when to see a vet immediately. It does not diagnose or prescribe.

Why am I being asked to wait before sending another message?

The free plan includes a limited number of chat messages per day so the feature stays sustainable. PawJourney Pro removes that limit entirely.

Subscription & billing

I subscribed but the app still shows "Go Pro."

Open Settings and tap "Restore purchases" — this re-syncs your subscription status directly from the App Store. If it still doesn't update after that, contact us below with the email on your Apple ID.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple, not inside PawJourney: on your device, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > PawJourney.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are handled by Apple under their standard App Store refund policy — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy & account

Where is my data stored?

Securely in the cloud, tied to your account — not just on one device. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

Settings > Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and data.
